## About

A gallery of pixel art from the **[Minecraft Galaxy](https://minecraft-galaxy.ru/ru/)** gaming project. A simple single-page app that keeps the collected works in one place, easy to browse.

## Features

- **Search** — a simple search of works by author or title.
- **Detailed viewing** — inspect pieces up close, with zoom and navigation.

## Tech stack

- **React**
- **shadcn** — UI components
- **Viewer.js** — image viewing
