## About

A simple single-page app built with **Vue** for a correct evasion-stat calculation in the **[Minecraft Galaxy](https://minecraft-galaxy.ru/ru/)** gaming project. It lets you assemble your gear — armor, its enchantments and in-game buffs — as you have them in-game to see the real evasion gain and judge how effective the next enhancement will be.

## Features

- **Gear builder** — a UI for armor, its enchantments and in-game buffs; parameters are set just like in the game.
- **Real evasion gain** — shows the actual stat gain and how effective the next enhancement will be.
- **Dependency graph** — plots the real evasion against the total evasion across all armor and marks the in-game soft cap, beyond which the real gain is tiny and further enhancement is pointless.

## Tech stack
