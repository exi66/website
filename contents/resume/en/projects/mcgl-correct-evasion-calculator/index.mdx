## Problem

Evasion in the game grows non-linearly: past a certain value a soft cap kicks in, and every next upgrade gives less. The in-game stats don't show this, so it's easy to spend rare resources on an almost useless upgrade.

## Solution

The evasion formula with the soft cap lives in pure functions. Players put together their gear and buffs and immediately see the exact and the rounded (as in the game) evasion, and a graph shows how much the next upgrade with each resource will give.

- Calculator state in a hook, saved to localStorage and validated with a zod schema; settings saved by the old Vue version are migrated on first launch.
- A Chart.js graph that marks the soft cap point.
- Light and dark theme shared with my other projects.

The project was rewritten from Vue onto a [template for static projects](https://github.com/exi66/starter): the shared theme, translations, settings, linter and CI come from it, while the formula and its tests stayed the same.
